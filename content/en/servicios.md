## What we do

### Wall tiling
![](/img/iconos/alicatados.svg)

We tile the walls of kitchens, bathrooms, utility rooms and other rooms, with ceramic or porcelain tiles, including large format.

We prepare the surface properly, set out the layout so that cuts end up where they're least visible, and finish corners and grout lines neatly.

### Floor tiling
![](/img/iconos/solados.svg)

We lay indoor and outdoor floors: living rooms, hallways, bedrooms, patios and terraces. Porcelain, including wood-effect.

With large tiles we use a levelling system so the floor is flat with no lips between tiles.

### Renovation
![](/img/iconos/construccion.svg)

General renovation work, so you don't have to coordinate several companies: complete kitchens and bathrooms, brick and plasterboard partition walls, doors and floors.

We also re-roof and waterproof roofs.

### Quality finishes
![](/img/iconos/acabados.svg)

The finishing touches make the difference: edges, skirting, corners, grout lines and where the tiles meet doors and fittings.

It's what you see every day once the work is done.
:::
