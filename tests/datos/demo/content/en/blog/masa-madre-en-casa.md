Keeping a sourdough starter is easy if you follow three habits.

## 1. Feed it at the same time every day

Regularity matters more than quantity.

## 2. Keep it cold if you don't bake every day

In the fridge it can go a week without feeding.

## 3. Pay attention to the smell

A pleasant, sour smell is a good sign.
