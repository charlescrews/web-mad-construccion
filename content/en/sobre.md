I'm Miguel Ángel Díaz and I've been in the building trade for over 20 years, specialising in wall and floor tiling.

![Large-format porcelain floor being laid, with the levelling system in place](/img/obras/colocacion-gran-formato-nivelacion.webp)

I set up MAD Construcción to take on complete renovations without losing what I do best: tiling done properly. I work in Loja and the surrounding area, and in Granada, Málaga and their surroundings.

## How we work

- We explain what we're going to do before we start.
- We take care over the finishing touches, because that's what you see every day.
- With large tiles we use a levelling system so the floor ends up flat.
- We give you a written guarantee and leave the site clean when we finish.
- The quote is free and with no obligation.
